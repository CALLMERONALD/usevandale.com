You can't answer every call. Your phone can still get answered.

Nights, weekends, long days on a job. An AI receptionist picks up, books the visit, and sends you the lead.

From ring to lead in three moves

  1. Point the calls our way. You set up call forwarding with your phone carrier. Your business number does not change.
  2. It picks up. The assistant tells the caller what it is, asks what's going on, and takes the details.
  3. The lead lands. You get an email as soon as the call ends. Every call is also logged with a summary.

Not for emergency dispatch or life-safety lines, outbound sales calling, or enterprise call centers.
